How Mobile Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

A little routine care is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
